# Date :
# Reg. No.: 212225040321 

# Automated Workflow Documentation
## College Symposium Management

# Aim:
To develop an automated workflow for managing a college symposium using AI-powered structured prompts. The workflow automates communication, planning, scheduling, documentation, and participant support activities, reducing manual effort and improving efficiency.

---
## Objectives:
1. Automate communication processes.
2. Generate professional documents quickly.
3. Improve planning and scheduling accuracy.
4. Reduce administrative workload.
5. Enhance participant experience.
6. Maintain organized records of symposium activities.

---
## Introduction:

Managing a college symposium involves numerous activities such as sending invitations, organizing meetings, assigning tasks, scheduling events, documenting requirements, and answering participant queries. Performing these tasks manually can be time-consuming and prone to errors.

By using structured prompts with AI tools, these activities can be automated, resulting in faster execution, better documentation, and improved coordination among organizing teams.

---
## Workflow Components:

The automated workflow consists of six major modules:

1. Email Writing
2. Meeting Minutes Generation
3. Task Planning
4. Project Scheduling
5. Requirement Documentation
6. FAQ Generation

## Module 1 – Email Writing Automation
## Purpose:

To automatically generate professional emails for symposium communication.

---
## Input Prompt:
```
Write a formal invitation email for a college symposium named "TechNova 2026" organized by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering. Include date, venue, registration details, and contact information.
```
## AI Generated Output:
## Symposium Invitation Email:

Subject: Invitation to Participate in TechNova 2026

Dear Students,

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering cordially invites you to participate in TechNova 2026, a national-level technical symposium to be held on 15 October 2026 at Saveetha Engineering College.

The symposium will feature technical paper presentations, coding contests, workshops, and project exhibitions.

Students interested in participating can register through the online registration portal before 10 October 2026.

For further details, contact the Symposium Coordinator.

Regards,

Organizing Committee

TechNova 2026

---
## Benefits:

1. Saves time in drafting emails.
2. Ensures professional communication.
3. Generates multiple email formats instantly.
